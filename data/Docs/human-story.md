## Key Concepts Quick Reference

- **Adam (آدم):** The first human and first prophet — created from clay (tin), then Allah breathed His spirit (ruh) into him.
- **Fitrah (فطرة):** The innate, natural disposition toward recognizing Allah and doing good — every child is born with it.
- **Nafs (نفس):** The ego-self with desires; the battleground of spiritual struggle.
- **Ruh (روح):** The spirit breathed from Allah — distinct from nafs in classical scholarship.
- **Qalb (قلب):** The heart — the spiritual center of perception and moral discernment.
- **Ibtilaa' (ابتلاء):** The divine test — life on earth is a test of faith, patience, and obedience.
- **Risalah (رسالة):** Prophethood — Allah's mercy of sending guides to every nation.
- **Khatam an-Nabiyyin (ختم النبيين):** Seal of the Prophets — Muhammad ﷺ as the final prophet.
- **25 Prophets:** Mentioned in the Quran by name — from Adam to Muhammad ﷺ.
