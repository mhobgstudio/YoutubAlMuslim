## Key Concepts Quick Reference

- **Mala'ikah (ملائكة):** Angels — created from light (nur), they have no free will, they always obey Allah.
- **Jinn (جن):** Beings created from smokeless fire (nar al-samum), given free will like humans.
- **Iblis (إبليس):** The forefather of evil — refused to prostrate to Adam out of arrogance (kibr), was expelled from Paradise.
- **Shayateen (شياطين):** Devils — disbelieving jinn who follow Iblis in misleading humans.
- **Kibr (كبر):** Arrogance — the sin of Iblis and the root of all spiritual diseases.
- **Munkar & Nakir (منكر ونكير):** The two angels who question the dead in the grave about their faith.
- **Hamalat al-Arsh (حملة العرش):** The Throne-Bearing Angels — among the greatest angels in creation.
- **Free Will (Mashiyyah):** Both humans and jinn have free will — this is why evil exists as a test.
