## Key Concepts Quick Reference

- **Qalb (heart):** The spiritual center of perception and moral discernment; the seat of faith, sincerity, and intention. "Indeed, in the body there is a piece of flesh... it is the heart." (Bukhari & Muslim)
- **Nafs (self/soul):** The ego-self with desires and inclinations; the battleground of spiritual struggle (jihad al-nafs).
- **Ruh (spirit):** The soul breathed from Allah; distinct from nafs in classical scholarship.
- **Three main Quranic stages of nafs:**
  1. **Nafs al-Ammarah** — the soul that commands evil (12:53)
  2. **Nafs al-Lawwama** — the self-reproaching soul (75:2)
  3. **Nafs al-Mutmainna** — the serene, content soul (89:27-30)
- **Tazkiyah (purification):** The process of purifying the nafs — the core of Islamic spiritual development.
- **Diseases of the heart:** Arrogance (kibr), envy (hasad), ostentation (riya'), anger, love of the world, heedlessness — and their cures per Ibn Qayyim & Ibn Taymiyyah.
