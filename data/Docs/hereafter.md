## Key Concepts Quick Reference

- **Tawaffa (توفي):** The taking of the soul at death — a gentle extraction for the righteous, painful for sinners.
- **Barzakh (برزخ):** The intermediate realm between death and resurrection — the grave life.
- **Munkar & Nakir (منكر ونكير):** The two angels who question the dead in the grave.
- **Ashrat al-Sa'ah (علامات الساعة):** The Signs of the Hour — minor and major signs before the Day of Judgment.
- **Dajjal (دجال):** The False Messiah — the greatest trial of the end times.
- **Ya'juj wa Ma'juj (يأجوج ومأجوج):** Gog and Magog —释放的末日民族.
- **Nuzul Isa (نزول عيسى):** The descent of Jesus (AS) — to kill the Dajjal and establish justice.
- **Qiyamah (قيامة):** The Resurrection — when all people are raised from the dead.
- **Al-Hashr (الحشر):** The Gathering — all humanity assembled on the plain.
- **Al-Hisab (الحساب):** The Reckoning — account of every deed, small and large.
- **Al-Mizan (الميزان):** The Scale — weighs good deeds against bad deeds.
- **Shafa'ah (الشفاعة):** Intercession — the Prophet ﷺ and others intercede on Judgment Day.
- **As-Sirat (الصراط):** The Bridge — over hellfire, every person crosses it at varying speeds.
- **Al-Hawd (الحوض):** The Pool — the Prophet's ﷺ pool where believers drink before entering Paradise.
