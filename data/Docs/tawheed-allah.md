## Key Concepts Quick Reference

- **Tawheed (توحيد):** The absolute Oneness of Allah — the core message of Islam and all prophets.
- **Three Categories of Tawheed:**
  1. **Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah** — Oneness of Lordship (Allah alone creates, provides, controls)
  2. **Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah** — Oneness of Worship (worship directed to Allah alone)
  3. **Tawheed al-Asma' wa's-Sifat** — Oneness of Names & Attributes (affirming Allah's names as revealed)
- **Al-Asma' al-Husna (الأسماء الحسنى):** The 99 Beautiful Names of Allah — each reflecting a divine attribute.
- **Al-Qalam (القلم):** The Pen — believed to be the first creation of Allah, used to write all that would occur.
- **Al-Arsh (العرش):** The Throne — the greatest creation, beneath which Allah's Footstool (Kursi) lies.
- **Al-Lawh al-Mahfuz (اللوح المحفوظ):** The Preserved Tablet — where all events are recorded before creation.
- **Al-Qadr (القدر):** Divine Decree — Allah's knowledge and writing of all events before they occur.
- **Four Levels of Qadr:** 1) Allah's Knowledge, 2) Writing (in the Preserved Tablet), 3) Divine Will, 4) Creation.
