## Key Concepts Quick Reference

- **Al-Bahr (البحر):** The Primordial Sea — the first thing Allah created, upon which He established His Throne. The water from which all creation originated.
- **Al-Arsh (العرش):** The Throne — the greatest creation of Allah, beneath which the Footstool (Kursi) lies. It spans the heavens and earth.
- **Al-Qalam (القلم):** The Pen — believed to be the very first creation of Allah, used to write all that would occur until the Day of Judgment.
- **Al-Lawh al-Mahfuz (اللوح المحفوظ):** The Preserved Tablet — where all events, decrees, and knowledge are recorded before creation.
- **Al-Kursi (الكرسي):** The Footstool — beneath the Throne, greater than the heavens and earth combined.
- **Pre-eternity (ما قبل الأزل):** The state before time began — when only Allah existed.
- **Mashiyyah (مشيئة):** Divine Will — what Allah wills happens according to the writing of the Pen.
- **Qadr (قدر):** Divine Decree — the writing of all events on the Preserved Tablet before creation.
