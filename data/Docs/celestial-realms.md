## Key Concepts Quick Reference

- **Jannah (جنة):** Paradise — the eternal abode of bliss for believers, beneath which rivers flow.
- **Jahannam (جهنم):** Hellfire — the place of punishment for disbelievers and sinful believers.
- **Firdaus (فردوس):** The highest level of Jannah — directly beneath Allah's Throne.
- **Al-A'raf (الأعراف):** The Heights — a barrier between paradise and hell for certain people.
- **Seven Levels of Jannah:** 1) Jannat al-Adn, 2) Jannat al-Firdaws, 3) Jannat al-Na'im, 4) Jannat al-Ma'wa, 5) Jannat al-Khuld, 6) Jannat al-Maqam, 7) Jannat as-Salam.
- **Seven Levels of Jahannam:** 1) Jahannam, 2) Laza, 3) Hutamah, 4) Sa'ir, 5) Sakar, 6) Jahim, 7) Hawiyah.
- **Seeing Allah (Ru'yat Allah):** The greatest reward of Jnah — seeing Allah's Countenance on the Day of Judgment.
- **Temporary vs Eternal Hell:** Scholarly debate — some scholars say hell is eternal for disbelievers but temporary for sinful believers.
